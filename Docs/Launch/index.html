Gifting Plus

Use RevenueCat, not promo codes. RevenueCat → Customers → search the person's Convex user id → Entitlements → Grant pro for a duration (a month, a year, lifetime). It shows in the dashboard, expires on its own, can be revoked, and needs no card. The server counts a grant as real (store: promotional), and the person's house turns Plus the next time they open the app (pro:refresh runs on launch) or within 6 hours (pro:sweep). Plus is the house's, so one grant covers everyone they live with.

Apple offer codes are the alternative for strangers (a free period that then renews, needs an Apple account with a payment method). PRO_COMP_USER_IDS is for the owner's testing only, never gifts.

Plus and homes: the rules

Plus belongs to a person, and it lights up the homes they are in. There is no plan stored on a home: a home is Plus while at least one of its current members carries Plus and that home is one their Plus covers. Server: lib/pro.ts (homeProStatus, coveredHomes, PLUS_HOMES_PER_PERSON = 2); live since 2026-11-19.

The cap: one person's Plus covers two homes

  • Covered = the first two homes the person joined, in join order (the order of users.home_id). A third home they are in gets nothing from them; it is still Plus if another member there pays and has room.
  • Leaving a home and coming back counts as a new join, so that home goes to the end of the order.
  • Comped people (PRO_COMP_USER_IDS) and RevenueCat gifts follow exactly the same rule as paying.
  • Settings shows "Your Plus covers 2 of 2 homes: …" to the person who carries it.

What happens when…

SituationResult
A free person tries to create or join a second homeRefused (multipleHomes), unless the home being joined is already Plus: then the house pays and they may join.
A Plus person creates or joins a 2nd homeAllowed; both homes are Plus on their one subscription.
A Plus person creates or joins a 3rd, 4th… homeAllowed (they have Plus), but that home is not covered by them. It is Plus only if someone else there pays and has a slot.
The Plus person leaves a homeThat home drops to free at once (unless another member covers it). Their Plus moves with them; the slot frees, so the next home in their order becomes covered.
The Plus person deletes a home (the last member leaves)The home and its data are deleted; the freed slot goes to their next home in order.
Someone else leaves a Plus homeNothing changes for the home.
Plus ends (cancelled, refunded, gift expired)Every home it covered drops to free unless someone else there pays. Nobody is removed from any home and nothing is deleted: what was written stays readable; new writes in Plus features wait for Plus. They keep every home they are in but cannot add another.
Two people in one home both payThe home is Plus while either covers it. If one leaves, the other keeps it Plus.
A free member of a Plus homeUses every Plus feature in that home. In their own other home (if any) they are free.

Who pays: the household

Apple sells a subscription to one Apple ID. NestZone's data belongs to a house. If Pro were per person, two people would open the same party and one would see a budget the other can't, which is worse than not having the feature. So any member with an active Pro subscription makes the whole house Pro. One subscription covers a house of five; that is the selling point, and it's why the other four care whether it renews.

  • No owner field needed. A house is Pro while some current member holds an unexpired entitlement. If the payer leaves, Pro leaves with them, with no stale plan_source to clean up.
  • A person in two houses lifts both. That's acceptable and simple; revisit only if it's abused.
  • The paywall says who is carrying it: "Murat's Pro covers this house."
  • Family Sharing: on. A family that shares purchases is almost always the same household.

Architecture

RevenueCat runs the purchase, the receipts and the renewals. Convex stays the only judge of what a house may do, because a client-side check is decoration: every old build and every modified client talks to the same server.

flowchart LR
  A[iPhone · RevenueCat SDK] -- purchase / restore --> B[App Store]
  B -- receipts --> C[RevenueCat]
  A -- "pro:refresh (after purchase, on launch)" --> D[Convex action]
  D -- "GET /v1/subscribers/{userId} (secret key)" --> C
  D --> E[(pro_entitlements)]
  F[cron: every 6h, near-expiry rows] --> D
  C -. "webhook (later, needs tunnel rule)" .-> G[POST /revenuecat]
  G --> E
  E --> H["requirePro(home) · homePro(home)"]
  H --> I[Pro mutations refuse]
  H --> J[notifyHome drops finance / calendar pushes]
  H --> K["pro:status (live query) → app gates"]

Why pull before push

HTTP actions live on the site origin (:3211) and the tunnel forwards only /.well-known/* there today. A RevenueCat webhook therefore needs a new ingress rule before it can work. Pulling instead needs nothing new: after a purchase or a restore, and once per launch, the app asks the server to check; the server asks RevenueCat with the secret key and writes the answer. A cron re-checks rows that are about to expire so a cancelled subscription ends on time even if nobody opens the app. The webhook is added later as a faster path, not a dependency.

PieceWhat it is

Old builds on the same backend

Every build in TestFlight talks to the one deployment, and none of them has a paywall. A refused write would show them "Something went wrong on our end". So enforcement is a switch, not a deploy:

  1. Deploy the backend with every gate written and PRO_ENFORCE unset. Behaviour is unchanged for everyone.
  2. Ship 1.12.0 to TestFlight, then the App Store. The new client gates itself from pro:status regardless of the switch.
  3. Once inbox:versionCensus shows the TestFlight households on 1.12.0, set PRO_ENFORCE=on.
  4. Existing TestFlight households get no grant. What they already put in Money and Calendar stays readable; adding more needs Pro, exactly like a lapsed subscription.

Reads never throw. A gated query for a free house returns its data, because read-only on lapse needs it, and old Today and dock screens would otherwise show alerts.

The paywall

An ordinary NestZone screen that happens to sell something, built the way the Nestie shop was settled: glass cards on the Backdrop, the theme's accent, SF Symbols, no countdowns, exactly one "Best value". It is a custom SwiftUI sheet on top of the RevenueCat SDK, not RevenueCat's hosted paywall, so it looks like the app.

  • Opens on what was tapped: "Money is part of Pro" with a still of the Money screen, then the four things Pro adds.
  • Two plans: yearly (Best value, with a 1-week free trial) and monthly. Prices come from StoreKit through RevenueCat, localised; the app never prints a hardcoded price.
