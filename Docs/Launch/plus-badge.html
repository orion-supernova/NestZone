The Plus badge

Every NestZone Plus feature wears the same small mark, whether the house has Plus or not. Without Plus it's the lock you already have. With Plus it's the same circle in the theme's accent with sparkles, the symbol Settings and the paywall already use for Plus. Same size, same corner, so upgrading looks like the lock turning into the mark.

Plus

Grey lock on frosted glass → accent circle with sparkles. Drawn as SF Symbols lock.fill and sparkles; the sketches here are stand-ins.

Where it shows

Why this and not something else

  • Keep the lock. It works, you like it, and "locked" is what a free house needs to hear. Swapping it for a crown on both sides would say "premium" but stop saying "you can't use this yet".
  • One shape, two states, instead of two different icons. A Plus house learns the same circle a free house saw. And because it changes in place, the moment someone upgrades every lock they've seen lights up where it is.
  • Sparkles, in the accent. Plus already uses sparkles (Settings row, paywall). It's the theme's accent, not amber, and it's the cluster rather than the single four-point star, so it can't be mistaken for the Sparkles currency (sparkle, amber).
  • No "+" glyph. On a button that already says "add", a plus badge reads as "add add".
  • No "PLUS" text pill. Too wide for a chip corner or a toolbar button, and it needs translating.

Details

  • One place for the look: PlusStyle (Design/ProGate.swift) holds the Plus symbol, the lock symbols and the colour. The badges, inline marks, the Settings row, the paywall, the read-only banner, the locked screen and the chore Archive swipe all read it. Purple sparkles later = change PlusStyle.tint to Palette.violet.
  • PlusBadge(isLocked:) is the corner badge, 16 pt. .plusBadge(isLocked:) puts it on a view. .proLocked(feature) now wears it in both states, so every existing gate gets the Plus mark with no call-site change.
  • PlusMark(isLocked:) is the inline version for titles and row ends: the segment names (Calendar, Money, Problems), "Create another home" and the Events teaser on Today.
  • The unlock uses .symbolEffect(.replace) and a Motion.pop spring (a badge is allowed a bounce). With Reduce Motion it fades instead.
  • It's decoration: hidden from VoiceOver and never takes a touch. Locked controls still say "Needs NestZone Plus" in their hint, as they do now.
  • Not badged: the Archive swipe on a finished chore (a swipe button has nowhere to put a badge, so locked it shows a lock in place of its glyph and with Plus it's the archive box), the read-only banner and the locked screen (free houses only).
  • Custom movie list chips: a free house sees the lock only when adding is blocked (removing a film is free). A Plus house always sees the mark.
