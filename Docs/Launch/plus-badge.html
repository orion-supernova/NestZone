The Plus mark

Plus is a word, not a symbol. Where there is text, a small PLUS tag. Where there is the brand, the NestZone house. On an icon-only control of a house that has Plus, nothing. The lock stays exactly as it was, and is the only glyph, only while the house is without Plus.

The tag, in every theme

The brand lockup

The Settings Plus card, the paywall hero, the Plus details header and the locked screen. A house.fill glyph, white, on the theme's accent fill in a continuous rounded square, then "NestZone" and the tag. I chose the SF Symbol tile over the AppIcon: the app icon is not drawable from code (it does not live in the asset catalog as an image), a bitmap of it would not follow the theme, and the house is already the app's glyph in the tab bar and Settings.

Contrast

Ink (.accentText, AppTheme.ink) on a wash of the theme's accent at 14%, over the surface it sits on. Text is 11 pt heavy, so it is held to the 4.5:1 body-text bar, not the large-text one. Rules

  • Locked: the lock badge and the inline lock, unchanged.
  • Unlocked, with text: PlusTag, on the text baseline, after the label. Segment titles, the Settings Plus card, Calendar settings rows, "Show adult titles", notification group headers, "Create another home".
  • Unlocked, icon-only (toolbar +, capture chips, movie list chips, the Add chip): nothing. plusBadge, proLocked and PlusBadge draw the lock or nothing.
  • Unlocking: locks fade out and tags fade in with Motion.fade, no bounce. Reduce Motion: no transition at all.
  • Native menus (a Picker in Customize) cannot draw a tag, so "Next event" there shows only the lock while locked.
  • The Sparkles currency keeps sparkle. Plus no longer uses a sparkles glyph anywhere.

API

  • PlusTag(font:): the capsule. Tokens in PlusStyle: tagFont (caption2 heavy), tagTracking, tagPadding, tagWashOpacity, tagWash(theme) (stored colours).
  • PlusMark(isLocked:): lock while locked, tag when not, cross-fading. PlusTitle("…"): a row title that gains the tag when the house has Plus.
  • PlusBrandTile(size:) and PlusLockup(tileSize:font:) ("NestZone [PLUS]", read by VoiceOver as "NestZone Plus").
  • PlusStyle.symbol is gone; lockedSymbol, unlockSymbol and brandSymbol stay.

Alternatives considered

  • Crown. Says "premium", not "Plus", and reads as a game or royalty; the lock-to-crown swap would also drop the "you can't use this yet" meaning.
  • Plus in a square. On an add button it reads "add add", and it is the generic "add" glyph everywhere else in the app.
  • Seal or badge. Looks like verification or a store promo, and it needs a corner to hang off, which clipped glass takes away.
  • Sparkles. The old mark: reads as magic or AI, and sits next to the Sparkles currency (sparkle) as a near twin.
