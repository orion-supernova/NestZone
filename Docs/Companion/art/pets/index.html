The kitten, before and after (v2)

v2 answers the art director's review of v1: fur is now part of the silhouette (notches and a few strands on the shadow side and the cheeks, never loose leaf shapes), the front leg is the coat's colour with a pale sock, the butterfly keeps clear of the head (which also removes the pink glitch on the bright face), and the duckling acts thirsty. The card, and the face

The grown kitten at card size by day, dusk and night, then two faces cropped from an 800-point drawing. The light comes from the sky's sun or moon, with a rim at dusk and cool shadows.

The four moods, acting

Bright (ears up, tail up with a curl, a head tilt, an open smile), content (a relaxed sit, a lazy tail), thirsty (ears flat and sideways, wide eyes on the little empty bowl, a low tail) and dozing (curled up, tail wrapped, eyes shut, the nightcap). The bowl exists only while thirsty. Then the light across the day.

Batch A: six pets at the kitten's level

The duckling, hamster, bunny, puppy, fox and hedgehog, each with its own character, through CritterPlan and the sitter's painter, plus one new body plan (waddler, the duckling's) and one new fur kind (SpineField, the hedgehog's spines). Every pet has all seven stages (a bundle or egg first, big-headed babies), the four moods as RULES-pets.md specifies, the sky's light, and idle motion. Each pet had two art-director passes: a screenshot, a list of rejections, a fix. Before is the app as it was (the hamster, bunny, puppy, fox and hedgehog were emoji sketches), pass 1 is the first complete drawing, v1 is after both passes.

All six together: -artSheet batch-a (grown, four moods), batch-a-faces and batch-a-faces2 (faces from an 800-point drawing), batch-a-night (morning, evening, night, moon). Each pet also has -artSheet <pet>, <pet>-moods, <pet>-close and <pet>-stages.

All six, grown, in the four moods

Duckling

Pass 1 rejected: the old painter was flat, with no light, no fur edge and a bead eye. Now a downy edge (FurEdge on body and head), a tuft, a bill in two halves that opens, webbed feet, a wing with feather scallops, the kitten's layered eye with a round pupil. It bounces rather than breathes. Dozing is its own pose: the head comes down onto the chest and the body squashes round. Pass 2 rejected: the white collar read as a hook across the face (thinner, shorter, lower); the down edge was spiky on the rump (shorter, wider tufts); the lower bill vanished (darker, so the closed bill reads as two halves).

Hamster

Pass 1 rejected: it read as a kitten with round ears: the coat's edge was spiky (fur 1.25, now .8), the cheeks did not bulge (cheek 1.7 widens the lower head and a cream pouch sits on each), the paws were two sticks, the seed was invisible, and the three big cream feet looked like slippers. Now it has no standing legs: short arms and two paws held up under the chin, a sunflower seed when bright, small feet, two buck teeth, a cream belly. Pass 2: the seed bigger and higher, nibbling.

Bunny

Pass 1 rejected: the ears ghosted (two bends faded into one another at rest) and the cotton tail was a star. Ears are built at five steps of flop (HeadGeometry.bunnyEar), standing when bright, easy when content, flopped sideways when thirsty and laid back asleep; the painter draws one bend at rest and fades two only while the mood moves. The cotton tail is a ball of fine fur that twitches. The nose shivers all the time and bursts now and then. Pass 2: integer ear levels for each mood, finer tail fur.

Puppy

Pass 1 rejected: kitten whiskers on a dog, ears that were flat flaps with a ring drawn on them, a thin dark-tipped tail. Now: whiskers off (the dots stay), darker furred floppy ears that lift at the root and flap when bright, a pale blaze, a big glossy nose, a thick tail that wags about its root when bright (a slow swish when content) with a pale tip, and a tongue that pants when thirsty.

Fox

Pass 1 rejected: the plume was a thin tail with a flame on the end (it is now fuller in the middle, bulge), the white bib smeared left of the leg, the hind foot was a black slipper, a stray fleck of fur on the back (fur 1.4 to 1.0), the tear lines were too heavy, and the sleeping tip merged with the white muzzle. Now: black socks and ear tips, a white bib and white cheek ruff cut from the furred outline, tear lines from the eyes, slanted amber eyes with slit pupils, a plume with a white tip, and a dozing fox that curls into its tail, the tail across its nose.

Hedgehog

Pass 1 rejected: a brown bear in a spiky hat: the face took the coat's dark brown (now pale, with a dark mask round the eyes), the spines were wide cut-paper shards (now narrow and shorter, six rows, tips only a little paler than the blade), a cream pillow of a belly and a long pink foot read as a tail (small feet, a small belly), and the hood had a hard edge (it fades). It curls into a ball when dozing, rings of spines from the rim in, a snout and one shut eye in the gap.

Batch B: the rest of the pets, and the owl redone

The deer, snow fox and polar cub are rows of the sitter (new: Ears.leaf, Marks.spots, legWidth, tailFluff, tailSize); the penguin is a species of the waddler that rocks instead of bouncing; the turtle (shelled) and the goldfish (swimmer) are two new body plans. Each has all seven stages, the four moods, the sky's light and idle motion, and had two art-director passes (a screenshot, a list of rejections, a fix; 15 to 21 in RULES-pets.md). Contact sheets: -artSheet batch-b, batch-b-night, and per pet <pet>, -moods, -close, -stages.

All of them, grown, in the four moods, and the light

Deer

Pass 1 rejected: ears that stood straight up made a rabbit, and a short muzzle made a puppy. Now broad leaf ears held out to the side, a longer narrower muzzle with a larger dark nose, thin legs with dark hooves, soft white spots on the back and haunch (and on the curled one), a small white flag of a tail. Dozing is a fawn lying curled up.

Snow fox

Pass 1 rejected: rounded mouse ears and a huge, detached, fin-shaped brush. The fox row again, in winter: pale blue shade (white only reads through its shade), thicker fur, a fuller brush with a nearly white tip, icy blue eyes, grey socks and ear backs.

Polar cub

Pass 1 rejected: fur tufts of a thorny coat down the haunch (now shorter, softer), a flat white-kitten face (a longer snout, a big black nose). Round, stout, small round ears, small wide-set eyes, stubby legs.

Penguin

Pass 1 rejected: a white face disc that filled the whole head, a brown blob for the gold neck patch, a flipper nobody could see. Now a small face disc with a dark crown round it, a gold crescent, a bigger flipper that waves when bright. It rocks from foot to foot (a foot lifting as it leans), never bounces.

Turtle

A patterned shell, built once: a dome, a pale rim, 16 scutes in three rows with growth rings. The neck is what acts: stretched and swaying when bright, sagging to the bowl when thirsty, drawn back to rest at the rim when it dozes, legs folded, the nightcap on the shell. Pass 1 rejected: it stood small and low in the card (drawn 18% larger now), and a dozing turtle hid its whole head (now it rests at the rim, eyes shut, legs folded).

Goldfish

The bowl is cached, the fish is what moves. Refraction: the fish is magnified and squeezed as it nears the curved wall, the gravel is seen through the water, a rim of the sky's light on the glass, a window highlight, and a pool of bright light on the floor away from the sun. Thirsty is the water going down with the fish hanging at the surface, gulping; dozing it rests on the gravel. Pass 1 rejected: a small bowl, a small fish, and bright and content were the same picture.

Owl, redone

Rejected: a smooth egg with a smooth oval head, no feathers, a brow that frowned, a tiny beak. Now painted feathers round the body and in each wing, the head's edge broken by feathers, a ruff round each face disc (the first was a sun of thorns), a softer brow, a bigger hooked beak. The earlier owl shots are owl-v2-*.

Duckling, the collar removed

The pale crescent under the bill read as a collar or a strange smile line (batch-a-v1-faces.jpg). Gone; the breast's gradient fades into the neck, which is what down looks like.
