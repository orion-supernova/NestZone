Life stages of the mammal pets

Before is the original build (the same pet scaled by stage); after is round 2: each stage a body plan of its own (a belly-down bean with a huge head, a chubby tuft-chested little one, a lanky long-legged young one) that can be named from the outline alone. Each row: before, after, then baby / little / young / grown in the four moods. Debug launch arguments are in RULES-pets.md, section 10.

Kitten (hero)

Baby: a round ball with a head as wide as the body, stub legs, slate-blue eyes, tiny folded ears. Little one: chubby, blue-grey-green eyes. Young: lanky, long legs, oversized ears and paws, thin tail, bold stripes, play-bow. Grown: unchanged.

Puppy

Baby: big round head, soft floppy ears, blue-grey eyes. Young: enormous paws on long legs, thin tail.

Fox

Baby: dark chocolate kit, short blunt muzzle, small round dark ears, no white tip. Little: rusty. Young: red coming through, ears up, white tip starts, long legs.

Snow fox

Baby: grey-cream cub with small round ears. Little: whiter. Young: white, pointed ears, lanky.

Bunny

Baby: a round ball with short ears. Little: ears 60%. Young: ears 86% on gangly legs.

Hamster

Baby: pink and nearly hairless, eyes shut. Little: fuzzy. Young: slim, nearly the adult coat.

Deer

Baby: big spots, long wobbly legs, large ears. Little: spots fading. Young: spots at 40%, lanky. Grown: spots gone.

Polar cub

Baby: tiny, round and sleepy, cream. Little: rounder. Young: lanky, whiter.

Hedgehog

Baby: soft white spines. Little: spines greying. Young: darker spines.
