Nestie trees: before and after

The other five trees brought to the pomegranate's level (RULES.md, sections 9-12). Left is the shared canopy painter, then the tree's own painter (v1), then the fix round (v2): trusses and spotted throats on the azalea, a tired tree that stays the same tree, no thread marks at the forks, a cheaper olive.
