Nestie colourways

After the finale a house can grow a favourite species again in a new colour: 0 is the original, 1 to 3 are three curated palettes for it (RULES.md, section 17). Each row is one species, the grown companion by day, content, cropped from a large drawing so the colour and the marking are real pixels; the second row is the same by night. Every picture is a screenshot of -artSheet colours-<species>-close from a simulator.
