The Nestie Lab

Reaching it (Debug builds only): tap Lab in the row under the card (flask icon), or the chip at the bottom right of the picture. The chip says Pretend house in orange whenever the card is not your real house. The sheet opens at half height: Today behind it stays live and scrolls, so you can watch the card while you change it. Pull it up for a scene of its own at the top.

Pretend house (the switch at the top) is off the first time. Off: your real house, served live, and the Lab only changes how it looks and writes nothing. On: a stand-in server on this phone with no network; everything below works for real, nothing reaches your house, and it is kept between launches. Switching resets the card.

Sections (pretend house on)

  • Presets: one tap sets the whole house, the day and the people: new house, thirsty pet at night, ready tree on Christmas eve, finale-ready, broke jar, rooftop at night, two people mid-vote.
  • Companion: any of the 39 species (portraits, grouped trees / plants / pets), colourway 0-3, stage, progress in the stage (slider), ready now, stage up (pays the jar), days grown, new seed.
  • Mood: force a mood, or make the house quiet / set last care / care streak and let the real rules decide; water it, finish a chore as you or as a housemate.
  • Time and place: hour slider and presets (dawn to night), a date picker, every holiday with its date for the hemisphere, north or south, time zone, a birthday today. These also work on the real house, as looks.
  • Scene: every spot with portraits, a backdrop, a wearable, everything at once, clear.
  • Jar and shop: Leaves and Sparkles sliders, just short of any price, own or unown anything, force the next purchase's answer (owned, short, unavailable, price changed), open the Sparkles store (test store, credits the pretend jar).
  • Album and finale: fill the album, all free species grown with the last ready, grown colourways, clear.
  • Moments: welcome gift, stage-up, ceremony, graduation under a new start, finale (two ways), a new one arrives, the vote opens, the holiday chip. Each sets the house up and plays through the real machinery.
  • The house: 1-3 people, open a vote, answer as each housemate, close it by deadline / everyone / someone leaving.
  • Server: answer time and offline, to see rollbacks and retries.
