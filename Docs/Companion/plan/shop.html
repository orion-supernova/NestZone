What each team decided in v8, and why

The reasoning behind v8. Version 9 kept the card, the make-up picture, "See Sparkle packs", the readability rules and the research. It replaced the plum room, the drawn coins and the lipped buttons with the app's own glass, SF Symbols and one pressed-in button.

Art and design

  • A solid plum room, glass only on what floats (the bar, the shelf switch). Text-heavy surfaces stay legible; glass on everything looked cheap.
  • Every thing on a lit stage. Emoji placeholders and drawn trees get the same "on display" look, like a shop window.
  • Drawn coins: a round Leaf coin and a four-pointed Sparkle gem. Emoji can't be tinted and differ between iOS versions, and ✨ turns to mush at small sizes. Two shapes never look alike, even in grey.
  • Buttons you press into. Violet spends from the jar; gold is real money, so the colour says which money is at stake.
  • One violet button per screen. Premium is restraint.

Motion

  • The shop grows out of the jar, so you learn where the shop lives (the video's first rule).
  • Switching shelves is instant; only the thumb slides. You do it all the time.
  • Paying sends coins from the jar into the thing, which squashes as they land: money visibly goes where it's spent.
  • Sparkles take a deliberate second to land, then you're taken back. Like Wise's "money arrived" moment, time added on purpose so people trust it worked.
  • No bounce on money, and no shake when something's too expensive: nobody did anything wrong.

Readability and UX

  • Nothing under 13pt, nothing shrunk below 90%, every colour pair at 4.5:1 or better. The app's violet is 2:1 on a dark sheet, so words use a lighter violet.
  • A card instead of system alerts. It shows the thing, its price in its own currency, and the jar before and after, with one clear button.
  • Making up Leaves is a picture (a split bar, "1 Sparkle covers 1 Leaf"), and "not enough" does the sum for you.
  • "See Sparkle packs", not "Get Sparkles", which sounded free.
  • Everything is tappable. Locked things say when they come; owned ones offer "Put it out".
  • Ask to Buy has its own words: "Waiting for approval".

Research

  • Real money goes through Apple's own sheet, with nothing of ours in front of it (Apple's guidelines).
  • Earned money never gets a "+": only Sparkles can be added, as in Finch.
  • Bigger packs get bigger art, not bigger promises (Supercell's pattern, minus the pressure).
  • No countdowns, decoy offers or "MEGA SALE" ribbons. Holiday things show their plain last day.
  • Later: EU guidance wants the real-money price near Sparkle prices. That's for the money step.
